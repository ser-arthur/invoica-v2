This notice explains what personal information Invoica collects, why, what we do with it and the choices you have. We
have kept it as short and plain as we can.

## Who we are

Invoica is a trading name of Kobby S. Arthur, a sole trader in the United Kingdom. In this notice "Invoica", "we" and
"us" mean him.

You can reach us at hello@getinvoica.com. Our postal address is [to confirm: the business address].

## Two kinds of information

**Information about you, as someone who uses Invoica or visits our website.** We decide how this is used, so we are its
controller under UK data protection law.

**Information you put into Invoica about other people**, such as your clients and their contacts. You decide what goes
in and what it is for, so you are its controller and we handle it on your behalf, as your processor. How we do that is
set out in our Terms, under "Your clients' information".

## What we collect about you

- **Your account:** your name and email address, and your password, which we keep only in a scrambled form that cannot
  be turned back into the password.
- **Your business:** what you enter about your business, such as its name, address, VAT or tax number, bank or payment
  details shown on your invoices, logo and colour.
- **What you do in Invoica:** the invoices, credit notes, payments, clients and items you create, and the emails
  Invoica sends for you (who they went to, when, and whether they arrived).
- **Keeping your account safe:** when you sign in we record the IP address and the kind of browser, with each signed in
  session. We use this to keep accounts secure and to limit how often sign ups and sign ins can be tried.
- **Your choices:** whether you prefer Invoica light or dark.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not buy information about you from anyone.

When paid plans start, payments will be taken by a payment provider. We will not see or keep your full card number.

## How long we keep it

- **While your account is open,** we keep what you need to use Invoica.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your information (much of it you can download yourself, from Settings)
- correct anything that is wrong
- delete your information
- limit how we use it, or object to how we use it
- move it to another service

Write to hello@getinvoica.com. We will reply within one month. If you are not happy with how we handle your
information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113), though we would
like the chance to put it right first.

## Cookies

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. Invoica uses only the cookies it needs to work:

| Cookie | What it does | How long it lasts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The sign in cookie | Keeps you signed in | 14 days, renewed while you use Invoica, or until you sign out |
| `invoica_org` | Remembers which of your businesses is open | Until you close your browser |
| `invoica_theme` | Remembers whether you chose light or dark | One year |

These cookies are needed for Invoica to work, or are set because you made a choice, so the law does not ask us to get
your consent for them. You can block cookies in your browser, but you will not be able to sign in.
